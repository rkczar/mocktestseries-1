RUHS Medical Officer 2026

Rajasthan University of Health Sciences, Jaipur conducts the Medical Officer Direct Recruitment Examination to fill Medical Officer posts in the Rajasthan Medical & Health Services. The written test is an offline pen-and-paper objective exam answered on an OMR sheet, set entirely on the MBBS curriculum, followed by document verification for qualified candidates.

Exam pattern

One OMR paper, one hundred marks

Paper Q Marks
MBBS-based curriculum 100 100
Total · 2 hours 100 100

  • 1 mark per correct answer and no negative marking, so every question is worth attempting.

  • 72 seconds per question on average — speed matters as much as recall.

  • Offline OMR sheet, marked with a ball pen. One bubble per question — double or incomplete shading is rejected, and there is no on-screen review, so answer transfer discipline is part of the exam.

Practising on screen is for learning and analysis. Before the real exam, take at least three mocks on a printed OMR sheet under full 2-hour timing — the printable OMR sheet ships with every full mock.

Syllabus

The whole MBBS course, in three blocks

RUHS sets the paper on the MBBS curriculum, so nothing is formally out of syllabus. In practice the clinical block carries the most weight, with case-based questions from General Medicine, Surgery and Community Medicine.

Block 01

Pre-clinical

  • Anatomy — gross anatomy, microanatomy, embryology, genetics, neuroanatomy
  • Physiology — general, nerve–muscle, blood, respiratory, cardiovascular, renal, endocrine, CNS
  • Biochemistry — biomolecules, enzymes, metabolic pathways, nutrition, hormones, molecular biology

Block 02

Para-clinical

  • Pharmacology — general, autonomic, CNS, CVS, chemotherapy, toxicology
  • Pathology — general pathology, haematology, systemic pathology
  • Microbiology — bacteriology, virology, mycology, parasitology, immunology
  • Forensic Medicine & Toxicology
  • Community Medicine — epidemiology, biostatistics, national health programmes

Block 03

Clinical

  • General Medicine and General Surgery
  • Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Paediatrics
  • Orthopaedics, Ophthalmology, ENT
  • Dermatology, Psychiatry
  • Anaesthesiology, Radiodiagnosis

Subject list compiled from RUHS MO syllabus references. RUHS does not publish a fixed marks-per-subject split.
